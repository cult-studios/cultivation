the pitch

sun-warm farming with a soft occult underbelly.

the surface is cozy: soil, chamomile, handwritten signs, market gossip. underneath, there's a stranger story about the plants, the woods, and what the town would rather not talk about.

01 · farm

grow the normal stuff. and the "normal" stuff.

carrots, chamomile, tomatoes, and a few plants you keep in the greenhouse. every season opens up new things to plant, brew, and quietly profit from.

02 · process

turn harvests into teas, tinctures, and favors.

drying racks, infusion stations, and ritual brews turn raw plants into things worth selling (and a few things worth hiding).

03 · choose risk

keep it chill, or invite the consequences.

play stress-free, or switch on a suspicion system where nosy neighbors and the law slowly start paying attention.

core loop

one more day. one more crop. one more questionable decision.

the loop is simple enough to feel cozy, but layered enough to get weird fast. each day is a small set of choices that quietly add up.

01planpick seeds for the season, your buyers, and how much risk you want.
02planttill, sow, water. the genuinely cozy part.
03tendweather, pests, and the occasional curious neighbor.
04harvestpull a crop you're a little too proud of.
05processdry, brew, and infuse into something worth more.
06sellthe shop, the cafe, the market, or a guy named Marlow.

sagebrook · thistleboro falls

small town. big opinions. bigger plants.

every location has a daylight face and a nighttime rumor. the farm is home base, but the town and the woods are where the story actually grows.

the town

sagebrook

cobblestone plaza, nosy storefronts, and a farmer's market that runs entirely on gossip. everyone knows everyone, for better and worse.

the woods

the whispering woods

older than the town, and paying attention. glowing fungi, instructive dreams, and paths that wander.

the cafe

the drowsy leaf

maple's place. tea, comfort, and the occasional mind-expanding cup. cozy by day, a little occult after dark.

the neighbors

warm, weird, and a little tired.

relationships are built around people who are funny before they're useful. everyone in Sagebrook has a secret, and most of them somehow involve your farm.
